Thus a root literal `ticket.case_insensitive` cannot satisfy the nested `ticket.case_insensitive` setting used by typed decode and required-setting validation. Nested mapping values retain distinct paths. `loadFromFile` validates the node tree before decoding and records those same structural paths.

## Hypotheses investigated

- **Aliases and replacement races could direct a writer to a different ledger or recreate its former path.** `AcquirePublication` locks and holds the selected directory inode, opens an `os.Root`, then checks root/descriptor identity against the admission path. Publication I/O is descriptor-relative. `ValidateLocation` checks the held directory's current physical location against configured roots and depth. Thus a rename within scope follows the held directory; unlink/replacement or move outside scope fails rather than authorizing writes through the stale name. Discovery canonicalizes physical paths, deduplicates aliases, and reports conflicting overlapping artifact policies. Existing focused tests cover alias deduplication and real rename/replacement.
- **Absent private recovery state could make interrupted bytes look complete or overwrite accepted history.** `BeginPublication` durably writes the private before-image journal before publishing the adjacent portable pending marker; artifact writes follow. Readers check that marker before and after reading under a shared directory lease, without initializing local state. If the marker exists but its journal is absent/unavailable, reads/recovery fail closed and preserve the marker and artifacts. Commit syncs members/directories before removing the marker. The native receipt confirms no-state embedded HTML/API/list/detail reads; focused publication tests exercise interrupted recovery and unavailable private state.
- **Missing source hydration could erase portable owners.** Backfill separates sessions by recorded source, refreshes only selected exact owners, retains other/unavailable owner collections, and marks incomplete refresh rather than treating missing sources as empty success. Targeted existing tests exercise Codex discovery failure, missing child retention, unavailable analysis, and cross-source preservation. Native receipt records exact Codex/OpenCode owner retention through archive, late refresh, and arbitrary external-root relocation.
- **Portable facts or old homes could be recreated by location changes.** Ledger-only identity is taken from recorded ticket IDs; explicit roots admit arbitrary/external configured homes. Ticket-based late refresh resolves the archived/current ledger and the native receipt confirms the former active home stayed absent after archive and external relocation.

Turn activity into investigation.

01 / Diagnose tool retries

Where did the run get stuck?

A tool keeps failing, or work passes back and forth between agents. Open the anomaly inspector and execution/delegation trace to connect the pattern to its context.

Your decision: repair the environment, narrow the task, or change the next instruction after inspecting the underlying evidence.

02 / Compare ticket effort

What changed between tickets?

Use ticket comparison and metric progression to examine role and model activity, tokens, cache use and recorded cost alongside elapsed and active duration.

Your decision: review an expensive investigation step, adjust task boundaries, or inspect a change in cache use. Account for task difficulty and missing evidence before drawing a conclusion.

03 / Preserve handoff history

Keep the context after the session ends.

A teammate joins from another machine or harness. Git-shareable ledgers and ticket summaries preserve recorded facts without copying your private session database.

Your decision: use the recorded history to plan follow-up work and identify what still needs investigation. Original sources are needed to recover missing detail; a shared ledger does not recreate a missing trace.

i

Evidence has limits. Keep them visible.

Anomaly verdicts and test/production ratios support investigation. They do not establish test coverage, productivity or code quality. A token count alone does not explain why a ticket was difficult.

Observed cost is a reported amount, including a reported zero. Partial cost is a known subtotal with missing contributions. Unavailable cost is unknown, not zero. Current Codex extraction does not establish USD cost. These are not verified invoice amounts or promised savings. Read cost semantics.

OpenCode live monitoring

Watch supported OpenCode sessions through the local dashboard. Follow turns, tools, token growth and agent handoffs as the work unfolds.

After the work

OpenCode & Codex generation

Generate offline ledgers from local harness stores. Source capabilities differ; unavailable detail stays unavailable rather than becoming a successful zero.

Across project history

Backfill & migration

Reconcile recoverable historical facts and explicitly migrate supported ledger schemas. Preserve unavailable history; original source data is needed for new extraction.

AgenticLens observes the work.

It is not an agent executor or orchestrator, a coding assistant, a hosted monitoring service, or proof of code quality. Your harness runs the agents. You interpret the evidence.

A small, local architecture

Your machine.
Your project history.

Local harness stores and Git provide inputs. Adapters read them; pure analysis derives the recorded metrics. Durable ledgers retain project facts for the CLI and the local embedded dashboard.

Local sources are authoritative when successfully refreshed. Unavailable sessions remain preserved in supported shared ledgers, across developers, machines and harnesses. Optional source detail enriches the record when available.

The native agentic-lens manager installs and selects the project’s pinned engine. The separate agentic-lens-engine performs telemetry work and embeds the dashboard. The installed application needs no production Node runtime.

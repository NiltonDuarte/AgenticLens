## Usability observations

The kit supplies enough operational detail to derive coverage, capacity waves, separate report ownership, persona loading and blocker precedence without inventing a voting rule. The revision-aware gate clearly allows integration verification before final consolidated approval, avoiding a circular prerequisite.

Practical limits remain: the actual harness may not expose named registrations, and a coordinator must explicitly discover this and use the documented fallback. The dispatch template does not itself populate exact feature entrypoints, deployment assumptions or revision evidence; those remain essential coordinator work. A hypothetical scenario without a diff cannot produce a genuinely executable review brief or a product approval, which is why this exercise preserves H1/H2 symbols and missing-input conditions. These are workflow prerequisites, not observed correctness defects in the guidance.

This single textual exercise shows usability for the supplied scenarios. It measures neither defect recall nor superiority to prior instructions, and contains no defect/control code trials or independent holdout. No false-positive or escaped-regression rate can be inferred.
